Privacy

Digimon Auto Chess — a free, non-commercial fan game. Last updated 5 October 2026.

Playing without an account

Your progress — the current run, your tamer profile and partner, your settings and the name you chose — is kept in your browser's local storage on your device. Nothing of it leaves the device, except what the leaderboard and VS matches need (below).

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you do, the game receives from Google only:

  • your Google account's ID — to recognise you when you come back, on any device;
  • your name — offered as your tamer name, which you can change.

It does not read your email, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account. Your game profile and save are then stored on the game's server (Cloudflare Workers) so you can continue on another device.

Leaderboard and VS

When a run ends, the leaderboard receives an anonymous ID of your device (or of your account, once you sign in), your tamer name, your best round, your wins and the board you finished with, so other players can see it and fight its ghost. In a VS match the other players see your name and board for that match.

What we don't do

  • no advertising, no selling or sharing of data with anyone;
  • no tracking across other sites.

Deleting your data

Clearing the site's data in your browser removes everything stored on your device. To have your account, profile or leaderboard entries removed from the server, open an issue at github.com/olkuznetsov/digimon-autochess/issues and we will delete them.
