## Music and art

- **Soundtrack**: original instrumentals made for this game by Sasha with Suno (Pro plan) in the
  mood of Digimon Adventure — *File Island*, *Digivolve!*, *Black Gears*, *Fallen Angel* and
  *Crest of Light*, two versions of each (`public/music/`).
- **Backgrounds**: File Island, Primary Village and the summer camp's aurora, generated by Sasha
  with ChatGPT from prompts written for the game (`public/art/`).
